# PT Anggoan Karya Narasena (Anggoan)

- Own product catalog and stock, with Wening as one product line inside it
- Leads from the Wening and Anggoan websites go into its CRM
- FA, HC, GA: minimal, handled through authorization in Pandu's ERP *(assumed)*
- Acts as GA provider for the group: safety gear, uniforms, cleaning chemicals
