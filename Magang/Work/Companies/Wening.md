# Wening

Soap brand made by [[PT Pandu Dwiloka Teknik|Pandu]] and sold by [[PT Anggoan Karya Narasena|Anggoan]]. Back to [[Companies]].

## Flow in the ERP
1. Anggoan orders Wening from Pandu. Anggoan's PO automatically becomes an SO at Pandu
2. Pandu ships it. Pandu's stock goes down, Anggoan's goes up, and an intercompany invoice is created
3. Anggoan sells it to stores and customers through POS, CRM and marketing, next to its other products
4. The holding view leaves out the Pandu to Anggoan sale. Only Anggoan's sale to outside customers counts as group revenue

## Production needs (at Pandu)
- Formula (BOM) per product: oils, fragrance, packaging and so on
- Batch number and expiry date per batch, for tracing and recalls
- QC per batch before it goes into finished goods
